🧭 How to Get There
🚌

Bus from Manila

Take a bus from Cubao or Pasay to Baler, Aurora. From Baler, ride a jeepney or van bound for Casiguran.

⏱ ~8–9 hrs total
🚗

Private Car via Baler

Drive from Manila via NLEX → TPLEX → Nueva Ecija → Baler, then continue north along the coast.

⏱ ~6–7 hrs
🛥️

Boat from Palanan

Travel by pump boat from Palanan, Isabela across to Casiguran Bay — a scenic coastal route.

⏱ ~2–3 hrs by sea
✈️

Fly to Palanan / Baler

Charter flights available to Palanan Airport (Isabela). From Palanan, take a boat or jeep to Casiguran.

⏱ ~1 hr flight + transfer
📅 Best Time to Visit
☀️ Mar – May
Dry season — ideal for beaches and waterfalls.
☀️ Nov – Feb
Cool and less humid, great for trekking.
🌧 Jun – Aug
Rainy season. Some roads may flood.
🌀 Sep – Oct
Typhoon season — avoid if possible.
💡 Travel Tips
Bring enough cash — ATMs are very limited in town.
Mobile signal is spotty; download offline maps before leaving.
Bring insect repellent for forest and waterfall treks.
Arrange local guides for Bulawan Falls and remote spots.
Stock up on supplies in Baler before heading north.
Respect local customs — Casiguran has indigenous communities.
